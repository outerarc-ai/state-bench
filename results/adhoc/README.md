# Ad-hoc result records

Records in this directory are **hand-written measurement and exploration
reports from real hardware runs**, not produced by state-bench tooling. They
do not follow the structured-record layout (regenerable `run.sh` + machine
readable `raw/` output) used by records directly under `results/`.

They are kept here, not in `docs/`, because they are dated measurement
records rather than guides. The provenance discipline still applies: every
number is labeled measured, spec, assumption, or simulated, upstream
references state their current state, and evidence not committed to this
repo is marked as external.
